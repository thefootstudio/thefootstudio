Pain & Movement Conditions

The common foot, ankle and lower limb conditions our podiatrists assess and manage, grouped by where you feel them. See Pain & Movement for our assessments and treatments.

Forefoot

Bunions (hallux valgus)

A bony bump at the base of the big toe as the toe angles towards the others, which can make shoes uncomfortable. We look at your footwear, foot mechanics and pain, and discuss options such as footwear changes, padding, orthoses and when a surgical opinion may be worth considering.

Forefoot pain (metatarsalgia, Morton’s neuroma)

Pain, burning or pins and needles under the ball of the foot or between the toes, often worse in tight or high-heeled shoes. Management may include footwear advice, offloading, orthoses and, where appropriate, injection therapy.

Retracted toes and toe deformities

Toes that curl, claw or retract, such as hammer toes and claw toes, which can rub in shoes and cause corns or pain under the ball of the foot. We look at footwear, padding, offloading and toe devices, and discuss when a surgical opinion may be worth considering.

Big toe joint arthritis (hallux rigidus)

Pain and stiffness in the big toe joint, especially when pushing off while walking or running. Footwear changes, orthoses and offloading can help reduce load through the joint.

Sesamoiditis

Pain under the ball of the foot behind the big toe, often in runners and dancers. We help with offloading, padding, footwear and orthoses, and refer for imaging where needed.

Plantar plate injury

Pain under the ball of the foot near the second toe, sometimes with the toe starting to drift or lift. Early taping, offloading and footwear changes can help settle it.

Midfoot

Arch pain and flat feet

Aching through the arch or inside of the foot, sometimes with the arch flattening over time. We assess how your feet move and take load, and use strengthening, footwear advice and orthoses or bracing where needed.

Tibialis posterior tendinopathy

Pain and sometimes swelling along the inside of the ankle and arch, which can lead to the arch flattening over time if left untreated. Management focuses on tendon loading exercises, with footwear, orthoses or bracing to support the foot.

Tibialis anterior tendinopathy

Pain at the front of the ankle and top of the foot, often worse with walking downhill, running or tight shoes. We look at load, footwear and lacing, alongside a graded strengthening program.

Midfoot arthritis

Pain and stiffness across the middle and top of the foot, sometimes with a bony lump that rubs in shoes. Footwear changes, lacing techniques, orthoses and padding can help reduce load through the joints.

Top of foot pain (dorsal cutaneous nerve entrapment)

Burning, tingling or numbness on the top of the foot, often from pressure of laces or tight footwear on the small nerves. We help with footwear and lacing changes, padding and other treatments to settle the nerve.

Cuboid syndrome

Pain on the outside of the midfoot, often after an ankle sprain or a change in activity. Management may include mobilisation, taping, padding and strengthening.

Accessory navicular

An extra bone on the inside of the foot near the arch that can become sore with sport or rubbing shoes. We help with footwear, padding and orthoses.

Rearfoot & ankle

Heel pain (plantar heel pain)

Pain under the heel, often sharpest with the first steps in the morning or after sitting, and sometimes called plantar fasciitis. The structures around the heel become irritated and sensitive, and load, footwear, strength and general health can all play a part. Management often combines several treatments.

Achilles tendinopathy

Pain and stiffness in the tendon at the back of the heel, often worse at the start of activity. A graded loading program is central, alongside load management and other treatments where needed.

Peroneal tendinopathy

Pain along the outside of the ankle and foot, often with walking on uneven ground or after ankle sprains. Management focuses on loading exercises, footwear and support where needed.

Haglund’s deformity and heel bursitis

A bony bump at the back of the heel that rubs in shoes and can inflame the bursa nearby. We help with footwear changes, heel lifts, padding and load management.

Ankle impingement

Pain at the front or back of the ankle when it bends fully, common in dancers and footballers. We assess the cause and use load management, mobilisation and strengthening.

Ankle sprains and instability

A rolled ankle that is slow to settle, or an ankle that keeps giving way. Rehab focuses on strength and balance, with taping or bracing such as the EXO-L where appropriate.

Kids’ heel pain (Sever’s)

Heel pain in active, growing children, usually during or after sport. We help with activity planning, footwear, heel support and exercises so they can keep moving.

Kids’ knee pain (Osgood-Schlatter’s)

Pain and a tender bump just below the knee in active, growing children, often with running and jumping sports. We help with activity planning, footwear, support and exercises so they can keep moving.

Lumps, bumps & nerves

Ganglions

A soft, fluid-filled lump, most often on the top of the foot or ankle, which can press on shoes or nearby nerves. We assess the lump, help with padding and footwear, and refer on for imaging or a medical opinion where needed.

Plantar fibromas

Firm lumps in the arch of the foot that can be uncomfortable when standing or walking. Offloading, padding and orthoses can help reduce pressure.

Nerve entrapments (including tarsal tunnel syndrome)

Burning, tingling, shooting pain or numbness when a nerve in the foot or ankle is compressed, for example behind the inside ankle bone. We work out which nerve is involved and help settle it with offloading, footwear changes and other treatments, referring on where needed.

Peripheral neuropathy

Reduced feeling, tingling or burning in the feet, commonly linked with diabetes. Regular foot checks, footwear and protective insoles help keep feet safe.

Movement & confidence

Shin pain

Pain along the shin with running or impact, sometimes called shin splints. We look at training load, footwear and how you move, and refer for imaging where needed.

Running injuries

From small niggles to injuries that keep coming back. A running assessment helps us understand your load, technique and footwear, and build a plan back to running.

Read more →

Bone stress injuries

Pain that builds with running or impact and is tender on a specific spot, including stress fractures. We look at training load, footwear and recovery, and arrange imaging and offloading where needed.

Kids’ foot and walking concerns

Flat feet, in-toeing, toe walking, frequent tripping or uneven shoe wear. We assess how your child is developing and let you know whether treatment is needed or whether to watch and wait.

Read more →

Growing pains

Aches in the feet, heels or legs in growing children. Ongoing or one-sided pain is worth checking, and we can help work out what is going on.

Arthritis in the foot and ankle

Stiffness and pain in the joints of the foot and ankle. Footwear, orthoses, bracing and exercise can help you stay comfortable and active.

Foot drop

Difficulty lifting the front of the foot when walking, which can lead to tripping. We work with your wider care team and may use an ankle foot orthosis such as the EXO-L UP or Richie Brace.

Read more →

Chronic pain

Foot or leg pain that has lasted for months. We take time to understand your history and goals, and build a plan around what works for you.

Ageing well and falls prevention

Foot pain, weakness and footwear can all affect balance. We assess your feet, footwear and balance, and suggest practical changes to help you stay steady and independent.
