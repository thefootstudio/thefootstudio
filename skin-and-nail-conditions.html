Skin & Nail Conditions

The common skin and nail conditions our podiatrists assess and treat. See Skin & Nails for our assessments and treatments.

Nails

Ingrown toenails

A nail edge that digs into the skin, causing pain, redness and sometimes infection. Options range from conservative care and nail bracing such as Onyfix to a minor nail procedure under local anaesthetic.

Read more →

Fungal nails (onychomycosis)

Thickened, discoloured or crumbly nails caused by a fungal infection. Treatment usually combines nail reduction with topical treatment and Lunula laser, with a GP referral for oral medication where appropriate.

Read more →

Thickened nails

Nails that become thick and hard to cut, from ageing, injury, fungal infection or pressure from footwear. Regular podiatry nail care keeps them comfortable and lets us check for underlying causes.

Involuted and curved nails

Nails that curl inwards at the edges and press into the skin. Nail bracing with Onyfix can gently reshape the nail as it grows.

Damaged or discoloured nails

Nails changed by injury, bruising, footwear pressure or skin conditions. We work out the likely cause, refer on where a nail change needs medical review, and can restore the look of the nail with Keryflex.

Psoriatic nail changes

Pitting, thickening or lifting of the nails linked with psoriasis. Regular nail care helps keep them comfortable, alongside care from your GP or dermatologist.

Skin

Corns and callus

Areas of thick, hard skin that build up from pressure and friction and can become painful. We gently remove the hard skin and look at footwear, padding and offloading to reduce the pressure that causes them.

Plantar warts (verrucae)

Warts on the sole of the foot caused by a virus, which can be painful to walk on. Options include topical treatments and, for stubborn warts, Falknor’s needling.

Read more →

Cracked heels

Dry, thickened skin around the heels that can split and become sore. We remove the hard skin and advise on moisturising and footwear to help prevent cracks returning.

Read more →

Athlete’s foot (tinea pedis)

An itchy fungal skin infection, often between the toes or on the soles. We confirm what it is and advise on treatment and on stopping it spreading to the nails.

Blisters

Fluid-filled pockets from friction, common with new shoes or sport. We look at footwear, socks and padding to prevent them coming back.

Sweaty or smelly feet

Excess sweating that can lead to odour and skin problems. We advise on footwear, socks and skin care to keep feet dry and healthy.

Diabetes & high-risk feet

Diabetic foot care

Regular checks of circulation, sensation and skin to help prevent problems before they start. We work closely with your GP and wider care team.

Read more →

Wounds and ulcers

Wounds on the feet that are slow to heal need prompt care. We assess and dress wounds, offload pressure and work with your GP and specialists.

Skin lesions needing review

Spots, moles or changes on the feet that look unusual. We check them as part of your skin assessment and refer you on for medical review where needed.

Kids

Ingrown toenails in kids

Common in growing children and teenagers, often linked with nail cutting and footwear. We treat them gently and show you how to help prevent them coming back.

Read more →

Warts in kids

Plantar warts are common in children. We discuss treatment options suited to their age and comfort.
