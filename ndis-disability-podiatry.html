Who we can see

We see self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants. We are not able to see NDIA-managed (agency-managed) participants. If you’re not sure how your plan is managed, check with your support coordinator or plan manager.

How we can help

  • Skin and nail care, including thick, ingrown or hard to cut nails, corns and callus
  • Custom orthoses and prefabricated insoles
  • Ankle foot orthoses (AFOs), including the Richie Brace and EXO-L UP
  • Ankle bracing for instability, and support for foot drop
  • Footwear advice and fitting in our shoe and movement shop
  • Walking, balance and mobility assessments in our studio gym
  • Kids with developmental or neurological conditions

Conditions we often see

  • Cerebral palsy
  • Autism and sensory differences
  • Down syndrome
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Stroke and acquired brain injury
  • Parkinson’s disease
  • Charcot-Marie-Tooth and other neuromuscular conditions
  • Intellectual disability

Your appointment

  1. 1

    Getting to know you

    We talk about your goals, your NDIS plan goals and how your feet are affecting your day to day, with your family, carer or support worker welcome to join.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    We look at your skin, nails, feet, footwear and how you move, taking the time you need.

  3. 3

    Your plan and reports

    We explain what we found, agree on a plan and provide the reports, quotes or letters your plan manager or support coordinator needs.

Unhurried, person-centred care

  • Appointments built around the person, at a pace that suits them
  • Families, carers and support workers welcome
  • Clear communication, with information written down to take home
  • Working with your support coordinator, therapists and medical team

If you have specific access or sensory needs, please call us before your appointment and we’ll talk through how we can make your visit comfortable.

What to bring

  • Your NDIS number and plan dates
  • Your plan manager’s contact details (if plan-managed)
  • Your NDIS plan goals, if you’re happy to share them
  • Any reports from other therapists or specialists
  • The shoes you wear most often, and any orthoses or braces you already have
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you see NDIA-managed participants?

No. We see self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants only.

Can my support worker or carer come to the appointment?

Yes. Family members, carers and support workers are welcome.

Can you provide reports and quotes for my NDIS plan?

Yes. We can provide the assessment reports, quotes and letters your plan manager or support coordinator needs, including for orthoses and ankle foot orthoses.

Is podiatry covered by my NDIS plan?

It depends on your plan and goals. Podiatry and items such as orthoses or braces may be funded, so check with your plan manager or support coordinator.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with a podiatrist.
