Acknowledgement Of Country

The Foot Studio acknowledges the Whadjuk Noongar people as the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we practise. We recognise that health, healing and the sharing of knowledge have taken place across these lands, forests, rivers and oceans for tens of thousands of years. We pay our respects to Elders past and present and honour the enduring connection to Country, community and wellbeing.
