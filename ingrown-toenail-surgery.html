Who it’s for

Could this help you?

  • A nail edge digging into the skin
  • Redness, swelling or infection around the nail
  • Ingrown nails that keep coming back
  • Curved or involuted nails
  • Kids and teens with ingrown nails
  • People with diabetes or poor circulation, who should have nail problems checked promptly

What happens

Your appointment, step by step

1

Assessment

We assess your toe, talk through what has been happening and look at contributing factors such as nail shape, footwear and how the nail has been cut.

2

Treatment options

Depending on your toe, this may be conservative care, nail bracing, or a minor nail procedure under local anaesthetic to remove the problem edge of the nail.

3

Follow-up

If you have a procedure, we'll give you dressings and aftercare instructions and book follow-up appointments to check healing.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

For a nail procedure we numb the toe with a local anaesthetic first. Our podiatrists are well trained and experienced in giving these injections, including to kids and nervous patients, and we take it slowly and as gently as possible. You'll feel the injection, and after that you shouldn't feel the procedure itself. We'll talk you through managing any soreness afterwards.

What does nail surgery involve?

Usually a partial nail avulsion: the problem edge of the nail is removed under local anaesthetic, and a chemical is applied to the nail root to reduce the chance of that edge growing back. In some cases the whole nail is treated.

Will it grow back?

The aim is to stop the problem edge regrowing, and in most cases it doesn't. There is a small chance (around 2 to 8%) of some regrowth. We'll explain this at your appointment.

How long is recovery?

Most toes heal within 2 to 6 weeks. You'll need to keep the dressing dry and wear open or roomy shoes while it heals. We'll give you a plan for dressings, sport and work.

Do you treat kids?

Yes. Ingrown toenails are common in kids and teens, and we'll talk you and your child through every option.

Can I claim on private health?

We don't offer on-the-spot claiming. If your extras cover includes podiatry, you can claim with your fund using your itemised receipt. Check with your fund about your level of cover.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with a podiatrist.
