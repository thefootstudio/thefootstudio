EXO-L ankle brace

A custom ankle brace that moves with you.

The EXO-L is made for your ankle from a 3D scan. It allows your ankle’s natural range of motion, but has a seatbelt-like function that can be set to stop unwanted movement before it causes injury.

Who it’s for

Could this help you?

  • Athletes and active people whose ankles roll or give way

  • Ongoing (chronic) ankle instability
  • People who want ankle support without taping every session

What happens

What to expect

1

Initial assessment

A thorough history and assessment of your ankle, previous injuries, strength and balance, to see whether the EXO-L suits you.

2

Biomechanical assessment and 3D scan

We assess how you move and take a 3D scan of your ankle so your brace is made to fit you.

3

Preparing your shoes

A local shoe repairer adds small patches to your shoes so the brace can anchor to them. This usually takes about a week.

4

Fitting and review

We fit your brace, show you how to wear it in, then review you about a month later. Footwear, orthotics and rehab are part of your plan too.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is the EXO-L different from a normal ankle brace?

It's made from a scan of your ankle and anchors to your shoe. It lets your ankle move naturally, then limits the movement that leads to a rolled ankle.

Can I play sport in it?

It's designed for sport and everyday activity. We'll talk through your sport and any rules at your assessment.

Will it work with my shoes?

Your shoes need small patches added by a shoe repairer so the brace can anchor to them. We'll explain this before your fitting.

What is the EXO-L UP?

A version of the EXO-L for foot drop and some neurological conditions, such as cerebral palsy. It is smaller and lighter than many traditional ankle foot orthoses.

Do I still need rehab?

Yes. A brace supports your ankle, but strength and balance work is still important, along with the right footwear. We'll build that into your plan.

Can I claim on private health?

Many extras policies include podiatry and orthotic devices. We don't have on-the-spot claiming, but we'll give you an itemised receipt to claim with your fund.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with a podiatrist.
