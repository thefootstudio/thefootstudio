Jesara Sabin

Practice Manager

Jesara is the Practice Manager at The Foot Studio and loves connecting with people from all walks of life. What she enjoys most about her role is getting to know patients, being part of their journey, and seeing patients get the care and support they need.

Jesara oversees the day-to-day operations of the clinic, with a strong focus on patient care coordination and supporting the team so that our podiatrists can focus entirely on patient care.

Outside of the clinic, Jesara enjoys spending time with family and friends, getting out and about, and making the most of life.

Acknowledgement Of Country

The Foot Studio acknowledges the Whadjuk Noongar people as the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we practise. We recognise that health, healing and the sharing of knowledge have taken place across these lands, forests, rivers and oceans for tens of thousands of years. We pay our respects to Elders past and present and honour the enduring connection to Country, community and wellbeing.
