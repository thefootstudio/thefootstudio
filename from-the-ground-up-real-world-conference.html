Our team  —  19 November 2026

From the Ground Up: Our Real World Practice Summit

On 30 and 31 July 2026 we did something big. We closed the whole clinic for two days so that everyone, and we mean everyone, from front of staff to podiatrists and nail therapists, could come together for From the Ground Up, our Real World Practice Summit.

“It was a really big investment from a business perspective to stop the whole clinic and bring everyone together. But I’ve never really focused on the numbers. I just see it as an investment in our team and my colleagues.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

Why a “real world” summit?

Investing in our staff’s education is really important to us. We support our team to keep up to date, attend conferences and keep learning.

Over the year, our team had travelled to conferences and courses around the country:

  • Laura and Sam attended the Sports Medicine Australia conference in Hobart.
  • Laura attended the national podiatry conference in Brisbane.
  • Laura and Jess attended a business leadership course in Noosa.

It was time to bring the research back to the clinic, which is why we called it our real world conference.

“It was a chance for everyone to come together and share their knowledge.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

Starting with our patients

Laura opened the two days by hearing from our patients. We were so grateful to the patients who recorded voice memos about their experience with us. It was a powerful way to start, and a reminder to keep patients at the centre of everything we do.

Two days of learning

Wellness 2 Age

We were so lucky to have Dr Kristy Robson from Wellness 2 Age as a guest speaker. Wellness 2 Age is a Charles Sturt University healthy ageing program for older adults living in the community. It combines physical movement with thinking challenges based on everyday activities, to support balance, mobility, memory and social connection together. It was inspiring to see what podiatry leaders are doing in this space, and how they’re thinking laterally about healthy ageing.

Changing the conversation around pain

Sam presented on chronic pain in podiatry, and how we can change the conversation around pain with our patients. You can find our free Understanding Pain education and toolkit on our website.

Scan to Solution

Smriti and Andrea presented on Scan to Solution, a local Learn and Live event run by the Australian Podiatry Association that they attended on behalf of The Foot Studio.

Real world advice in the consult room

We collaborated with Mark from Kinima Physio on real world advice for the consult room. His energy was incredible, and so uplifting.

Footwear updates and “shoe and tell”

We had footwear updates from brands we stock in our footwear and essentials shop, including Altra, and a Ziera “shoe and tell”.

Running assessments and treating the athlete

Aoife, who has a special interest in running and athletes, led a hands-on practical session on running assessments and treating the athlete, using our treadmill and gym space.

The Resilience Project

“I was told that this is normal for larger organisations. But when I heard about the content, it didn’t matter to me that we’re a small family business. I had to get this for our team.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

The Resilience Project webinar introduced our team to GEM: gratitude, empathy and mindfulness. Since the conference, we’ve built GEM into our daily huddle and our everyday routines.

“I’ve always said when you get great people in a room, incredible things happen.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

Great food, lots of time to connect and learn.

What actually works

The conference was such a hit that everyone was still talking about it months later. It was about bringing the research back to clinical practice, and learning from each other about what actually works and is relevant for our clients and the community we live in.

“We will definitely do it again next year.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio
