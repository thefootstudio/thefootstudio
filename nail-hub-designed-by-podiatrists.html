Beauty & relaxation  —  19 November 2026

Designed by Podiatrists: Inside Our Nail Hub

Our nail hub sits directly across the hallway from our podiatry rooms. It’s a place to relax and enjoy a beautiful manicure or pedicure, and every step of every treatment has been designed by podiatrists.

It started with our clients

The nail hub was never part of a grand business plan. It came from our clients. Laura’s earlier clients would often say to her, “Laura, we need a place that’s safe and clean, where we can have a beautiful, relaxing treatment.”

Always listening to her clients, Laura eventually decided: why not? The nail hub is now almost four years old, and it’s still shaped by the people who use it.

Every step designed by podiatrists

Our nail therapists are trained by our podiatrists, and every step within each item on the nail hub menu has been designed with healthy skin and nails in mind.

As Laura says with a laugh:

“We tried to keep it simple, but we couldn’t help going into the detail. It’s the detail and the options that make it so appealing to everyone.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

From how nails are tidied to how cuticles are cared for, nothing is done just because it’s always been done in salons. Each step has a reason behind it.

How do you build a nail hub centred around healthy nails?

“It’s simple really. Infection control, good product choice, and careful training, with every step designed by podiatrists. It’s never perfect, but it’s pretty close to it.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

Infection control

The nail hub is held to the same clinical infection control standards as our podiatry rooms. That includes safe reprocessing of instruments, hand hygiene, personal protective equipment, and single use items where appropriate.

Good product choice

Every product in the nail hub has been chosen by our podiatry team. That’s why we use Dazzle Dry rather than traditional gels, which can weaken the nail and often need harsh removal.

Careful training

Our nail therapists are trained step by step by our podiatrists, so every treatment is done the same careful way.

Podiatrists across the hall

If a nail therapist notices a discoloured nail, or anything painful or unusual, our podiatrists are right across the hallway and can review it straight away.

Why we chose Dazzle Dry

Dazzle Dry was founded by Dr Vivian Valenty, a bio-organic chemist. After discovering what was in many everyday nail products, she set out to create a healthier alternative that still performs, and launched her own nail care line in 2007. She believes beauty and wellness go hand in hand, which is exactly how we see it too.

Dazzle Dry is a four-step polish system:

  • Nail Prep cleanses the nail and adds moisture
  • Base Coat grips the colour
  • Lacquer in over 150 shades
  • Top Coat seals it with a glossy, fast-drying shine

According to Dazzle Dry:

  • It dries in around five minutes, with no UV or LED lamp.
  • It’s designed to last 7 to 14 days on fingers, and up to 30 days on toes.
  • It removes with ordinary nail polish remover, with no soaking or filing off.
  • It’s 100% vegan and cruelty-free.
  • It’s formulated without ingredients such as formaldehyde, toluene, camphor and the monomers used in gel products.

For us, it means a long-lasting, glossy finish without the lamp, and without the harsh removal that can be tough on nails.

“As a podiatrist, I’m really happy with how our clients’ nails are looking when they come to remove it. And yes, I’m personally a regular at the nail hub myself.”

Laura Kelly, Director and Podiatrist, The Foot Studio

Your own base coat

For best practice, we recommend purchasing your own Dazzle Dry base coat, or your own Dazzle Dry kit with the whole system. If you’d prefer not to, we screen every client, and only apply our base coat to clients with healthy nails.

Nail hub or podiatry?

If your feet and hands are healthy and you’d like a relaxing treatment, the nail hub is your place. If something hurts or looks wrong, like a thick, ingrown, fungal or discoloured nail, book with a podiatrist first. Not sure? Start with podiatry, and if all is well, we’ll point you across the hall.

See the nail hub menu, book a nail therapist beauty appointment, or read about our skin and nail podiatry.

Nail hub treatments are beauty treatments by our nail therapists. They don’t include podiatry treatment, and private health rebates don’t apply.
