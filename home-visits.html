Who it’s for

Could this help you?

  • Older adults who find it hard to get out
  • Limited mobility, or recovering from illness or surgery
  • People living with disability, including self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants
  • Thick, curved or hard to cut toenails
  • Corns, callus and dry or cracked heels
  • Regular foot checks for diabetes
  • Family and carers arranging care for someone at home

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which suburbs do you visit?

Pop your suburb in the form or give us a call, and we’ll let you know if we can visit you.

Is there a call-out fee?

Yes. The call-out fee depends on the podiatrist’s travel time to and from your home. You can see our current fees on our Pricing Policy page.

Can a family member or carer book for me?

Yes. Just let us know their name and relationship to you, and the best person to call.

Do you see NDIS participants at home?

Yes. We see self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants. Please have your plan details handy when we call.

Can I claim on private health?

We don't offer on-the-spot claiming. If your extras cover includes podiatry, you can claim with your fund using your itemised receipt. Check with your fund about your level of cover.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with a podiatrist.
