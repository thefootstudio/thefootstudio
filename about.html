From the ground up!

Podiatry done differently

Podiatry done well, taken to its fullest potential through thoughtful environmental design.

The Foot Studio is a modern podiatry practice in Claremont, Western Australia. From the moment you connect with us, you’ll find calm spaces, unhurried appointments, and clinicians who take the time to listen.

We support people of all ages and abilities from children and athletes through to parents, grandparents and older adults, whether you’re active, managing a condition, recovering from injury, or simply wanting to move and feel better in everyday life.

Every care plan is shaped around you: your goals, your pace, your lifestyle, your preferences and what matters most to you.

Health from the ground up.

Our Story

The Foot Studio began with a simple need: a light-filled, spacious environment where both plants and people could thrive, doing podiatry to its fullest. Environmental design was a key element from the very start.

From humble beginnings, that dream has grown into a place where clients, team members and our community can thrive. We value treating people like people: connecting, listening and understanding what each person wants to achieve. Every individual is different, and so is every solution.

No matter who comes through our doors, our mission to provide high quality best practice podiatry treatment and recommendations that they deserve.

Our purpose is to help people live happier, healthier lives from the ground up. We believe the role of podiatry goes far beyond feet. It’s about helping people live the lives they want to live, whether that means walking without pain, keeping up with grandkids, returning to sport, or simply feeling more comfortable every day.

Best health care for all means creating inclusive pathways that can be tailored to each person’s goals, health, preferences and needs.

Health care can so often feel rushed and busy. We enjoy slowing things down and doing them properly, because that’s how we get to truly look after people in an ever busy world.

We are proud to care for our local community in Claremont and across Perth, while also supporting people in regional, rural and remote communities throughout Western Australia and across Australia via telehealth.

Vision

Our vision is for every Australian to have access to high quality podiatry care, because everyone deserves to live their happiest, healthiest life possible.

Mission

To provide high quality, patient-centred, empowering podiatry care across all areas of health care, including private practice, aged care, and rural and remote communities.

Our Values

Our core values guide everything we do, ensuring every person receives the highest care and attention. We are committed to:

Be a Good Human

We lead with kindness, respect and empathy, and we go above and beyond for our clients and for each other.

  • We go above and beyond for our clients and team
  • We are inclusive, welcoming and supportive
  • We show up and give back to our community

Always Learning

No matter how experienced we are, none of us knows it all. We love learning, while staying quietly confident in what we know.

  • We keep learning at every stage of our careers
  • We learn from and mentor each other
  • We work collaboratively with other health professionals and across the health system, never in silos
  • We embrace new evidence and new ideas

Do It Properly

We take pride in delivering evidence-informed, high-quality care. We pay attention to detail, hold ourselves to high standards and always do what’s right.

  • We are professional, thorough and accountable
  • We strive for excellence in everything we do
  • We put patient safety and outcomes first

Sustainability

We care about health that lasts, for people, for our profession and for the planet.

  • We move well, from walking meetings to the way our studio is designed
  • We use environmental design to create calm, light-filled spaces
  • We reduce our environmental footprint wherever we can
  • We build a sustainable podiatry profession through teamwork and mentoring

These values shape every interaction and treatment we provide, ensuring you receive compassionate, professional and effective care. If we’ve fallen short, or you’d like to share a compliment or suggestion, please let us know.
